# C3 Goal Area Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Robots park inside a 0.30 × 0.90 m red goal area (queueing 0.10 m apart) and robots exploring when the goal is found turn back at once when the mapped way to the goal is shorter behind them.

**Architecture:** SDF gains `goal_area`; `MazeSolver` gets `GOAL_PARKING` and handles `GOAL_ROUTE` orders; `TeamLedger.goal_routes()` decides turn-back per robot at `set_goal`.

**Tech Stack:** ROS 2 Humble rclpy, unittest/pytest, Gazebo Fortress.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-c3-goal-area-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Goal area centre (−1.25, 0.15), size 0.30 × 0.90 × 0.002 at z 0.0025; lines `(-1.25,0.0)` and `(-1.25,0.5)` raised to z 0.004; old 0.14 m red square removed.
- `GOAL_PARK_GAP_M = 0.10`; parking speed `APPROACH_SPEED`; arrival only when stopped with a red sensor.
- `TEAM_GOAL_RADIUS_M` / `TEAM_ASSEMBLY_RADIUS_M` and their block removed.
- No speed/gain tuning; real-robot notes to `toRealbot.md`.

## Review Focus
1. Second robot entering the area must not trigger a second GOAL / reset the goal — Task 2 `test_goal_reported_once`.
2. Parking robot must ignore junction triggers, head-on, walls and line recovery — Task 2 `test_parking_ignores_peer_ahead_head_on`.
3. A robot near the goal on another line must not count as arrived — Task 2 `test_near_goal_without_red_is_not_arrived`.
4. GOAL_ROUTE turn_back while RETURNING or at a junction must be ignored — Task 3 `test_goal_route_ignored_when_returning`.
5. goal_routes must skip the finder and returning robots — Task 3 `test_goal_routes_skip_finder_and_returning`.
